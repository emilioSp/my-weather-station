# Temperature units

## Problem

The application currently supports Celsius only.

## Who needs it solved

People who use the weather station web app and prefer Fahrenheit.

## Wanted outcome

Users can view temperatures in Celsius or Fahrenheit.

## Known constraints

- None recorded yet.

## Maintainer decision, 2026-09-01

Use an accessible Celsius/Fahrenheit radio group in the WeatherStation header, next to the refresh control.
